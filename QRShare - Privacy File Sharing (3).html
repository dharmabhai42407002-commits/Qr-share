Privacy-First Architecture

QRShare is built on the philosophy of zero trust, minimal telemetry, and auto-purging temporary storage.

AES-256 Encryption

Files are encrypted before storing. Access PINs act as secondary decryption keys so unauthorized users cannot access payloads.

Auto Self-Destruct

Configurable expiration timers ensure your data automatically purges from servers once downloaded or expired.

No Tracker Policy

No account registration, no tracking cookies, and no IP logging. Anonymous peer-to-peer style temporary transfers.
